## Notes

**This form collects only what the proof requires**: who, when, which text, by
what means. It asks for no personal address, no identity document and no
technical data.

**The fingerprint replaces archiving a copy**: it proves, years later, which
exact text was accepted, without keeping the document with each participant.

A **substantial change** to the Terms calls for a **fresh acceptance**: the
earlier one covered a different text, and its fingerprint evidences that.
